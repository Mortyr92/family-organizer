- New: the Chores menu is replaced by "Taakjes & Routines" with a card per family member (name and avatar on top, their tasks and routines below) plus a "free to pick up" card for unassigned items.
- New: routines recur daily, weekly or monthly with intervals and dayparts (morning, afternoon, evening or custom); default daypart times are configurable in Settings.
- New: task lifecycle statuses: active, upcoming, late, retry, expired and done. Retry windows after the deadline are supported (default one hour).
