Family Organizer 0.3.0

Completes the Cozi-style feature set inside Home Assistant: alongside the
existing Calendar, Shopping/Meals, Chores, Recipes and Settings pages the panel
now offers Today, To Do, Journal and Birthdays. Implemented with original code
and Family Organizer branding. Not affiliated with Cozi; no Cozi account,
service, subscription or telemetry is used.

Highlights
- Today page: agenda, upcoming events, planned meals, shopping, to-dos,
  chores, latest journal entry and birthday callouts in one glance.
- To Do: multiple task lists with due dates, assignees, notes and
  "clear completed"; every list is also a Home Assistant todo entity.
- Journal: dated family moments with tagged members and photo URLs.
- Birthdays: per-person birthday field with countdown and age being turned.
- Weekly meal planner moved onto the Meals page next to the recipe box.
- Quick add now covers to-dos and journal entries.
- New `manage_todos` and `manage_journal` capabilities with role presets and
  per-person overrides.

Upgrade
- No uninstall or reset is required. Storage version is unchanged; the new
  todos and journal stores start empty. Restart Home Assistant after updating.
